# Physical illustrations

Six vector diagrams summarize the main physical settings studied in this repository. Each figure is a schematic, with equations and labels chosen to distinguish implemented calculations from interpretations and proposed work.

Displayed equations and indexed diagram labels are typeset with LaTeX and embedded as vector outlines so they remain sharp when zoomed. Each equation outline preserves its LaTeX source in its SVG title.

1. [Closed polyhedron from face areas and normals](01-closed-polyhedron.svg) — outward face-area vectors close, and the classical Minkowski data determine a convex polyhedron up to translation. Source: [T6 Minkowski reconstruction](../../memory-bank/implementation-details/T6-minkowski-polyhedron.md), [volume and shape chronology NS12–16](../../notes/numerical-studies.md#ns12).
2. [FL states and face channels](02-fl-states-and-face-channels.svg) — spinor-labelled fixed-area states superpose face-area partitions; graph hopping can activate additional channels. The free large-area run recurs, while the small-area interacting pilot is preparation-dependent. Sources: [Fock-space construction](../../memory-bank/implementation-details/fock-space-construction.md), [face activation and equilibration notes](../../notes/face-energy-equipartition.md), [numerical chronology NS32–33](../../notes/numerical-studies.md#ns32).
3. [Positive volume and Hamiltonians](03-volume-and-hamiltonian.svg) — RS and AL positive roots are distinct from a signed-mean proxy. In the four-site baseline, volume is an observable of a hopping-plus-repulsion Hamiltonian, not a term in it. Sources: [volume definitions and limits](../../memory-bank/implementation-details/volume-operator.md), [four-site Hamiltonian study](../../notes/hamiltonian-studies.md), [numerical chronology NS21–23 and NS27](../../notes/numerical-studies.md#ns21).
4. [Sewing and singlet exchange](04-sewing-and-singlet-exchange.svg) — tensor contraction identifies boundary links; the T9a Hamiltonian transfers singlet area between still-separate closed copies. Sources: [constructive sewing probe](../../notes/constructive-sewing-probe.md), [singlet-exchange model](../../notes/t9a-singlet-exchange.md), [numerical chronology NS25 and NS30](../../notes/numerical-studies.md#ns25).
5. [Thermal two-copy closure](05-thermal-two-copy-closure.svg) — a conjugate two-copy squeeze preserves combined dual closure; separate singlet projection gives ordinary closure in each copy while area fluctuates. Heating increases projected area, positive RS volume, and entanglement for the studied area-one reference. Sources: [heating and exchange note](../../notes/thermal-geometry-and-singlet-exchange.md#section-16), [closed-reference entanglement](../../notes/thermal-entanglement.md), [numerical chronology NS28–31](../../notes/numerical-studies.md#ns28).
6. [Classical rotors and quantum continuation](06-classical-rotors-and-quantum-proposal.svg) — a zero-total-angular-momentum rotor ring is separate from the exact nonzero-total-momentum planar-pair pendulum. The documented pair benchmark is proposal-level; a four-planar-rotor calculation at exactly zero total momentum is in progress and is not represented by results here. Sources: [classical rotor equilibration](../../notes/classical-rotor-equilibration.md), [few-rotor transfer](../../notes/few-rotor-energy-transfer.md), [coupling crossover](../../notes/rotor-coupling-crossover.md), [October 16 handoff](../../memory-bank/sessions/2026-10-16-equilibration-and-rotors-summary.md), [numerical chronology NS34–36](../../notes/numerical-studies.md#ns34).

## Caption and scope notes

The tetrahedron and face-channel graph are explanatory drawings, not plots of a particular numerical trajectory. RS/AL values are in repository project normalization unless a study states otherwise; physical regularization factors have not been selected. Sewing has verified tensor amplitudes, but metric shape and volume remain open. Thermal squeeze temperature is a preparation parameter and does not by itself establish Gibbs equilibrium. Classical rotor transfer depends on the tested graph, preparation, coupling, and observation time; the proposed quantum pair still needs a specified total-angular-momentum sector and matched initial state.
