The quantum pilot uses a four-site ring with initial momentum state $|m,-m,0,0\rangle$ for $m=2,4$, with uniform-angle classical comparisons. The results are recorded as NS34–NS37. Long-time equilibration and broader preparation dependence were not acceptance criteria for this finite-time pilot and remain possible follow-up work under T11.
