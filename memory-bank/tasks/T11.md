### Candidate Hamiltonians and prepared-FL dynamics — 2026-10-11

In the 105-dimensional $K=4$, total-spin-zero four-face space, the baseline
$H_0$ contains complete-graph area exchange and the preferred on-site
repulsion $\frac{U}{2}\sum_i n_i(n_i-1)$. The complete graph represents
tetrahedral face adjacency with equal hopping on its six edges; the ring is a
sparse-hopping control. Two candidates were tested:

$$
H_V=H_0+g_VV_{\mathrm{RS}},\qquad
H_\chi=H_0+\lambda_\chi Q_\chi.
$$

For the closed four-face sector, $Q_\chi=4q_{012}$ and
$V_{\mathrm{RS}}=2\gamma^{3/2}\sqrt{|Q_\chi|}$ to a matrix residual below
$10^{-15}$. The positive-volume term responds to the magnitude; the signed
term also selects orientation. This relation makes them related probes in
this sector, not independent evidence for a uniquely physical interaction.
The full scan contains 62 parameter settings. Complete-graph runs span
$U/t=0,1,5,20$ and dimensionless couplings $\alpha=-2,-1,-0.5,0,0.5,1,2$;
the ring is a limited $U/t=5,20$, $\alpha_V=-1,0,1$ control.

All five equal-input-area FL references ($x=1/\sqrt3$,
$\varphi=90^\circ,60^\circ,30^\circ,10^\circ,0^\circ$) were propagated under
the baseline and both signs of each candidate at $U/t=5,20$: 50 trajectories.
Exact energy-basis phases were used, with return probability, volume, face
areas, angular correlations, and signed-grasp readouts. Norm, energy, total
area, and singlet-closure residuals were retained. The independent verifier
performed 1,510 checks; its largest residual was $1.07\times10^{-12}$.

At complete-graph $U/t=20$, the baseline ground mean volume is $0.033487$;
$H_V$ gives $0.601241$ at $\alpha_V=-1$ and $0.031332$ at $+1$. Either sign
of $H_\chi$ selects mean volume $0.601235$ while reversing the signed grasp.
The thermal response and time-series protocol are in the [candidate report](../../results/t11-candidate-hamiltonians/README.md);
the focused positive-volume scan is in the [volume-coupling report](../../results/t11-volume-coupling/README.md).

The couplings remain candidate parameters. Their physical derivation,
equal-hopping justification beyond the stated tetrahedral adjacency model,
and volume calibration remain open.

## Scope limits

The first pilot has only four available sites. Both four-site graphs induce connected subgraphs on every three-site subset, so they use the same four volume terms; the graph comparison changes the hopping terms. The calculated positive RS operator is not physically calibrated, and the pilot does not establish a thermodynamic phase, a larger-graph cancellation rule, or a graph-reordering amplitude. Strict one-boson-per-site dynamics are not generated by ordinary hopping alone.
