## Description
Catalogue the complete closed quantum state space at a given total linear area $K$, allowing the number of active faces $N$ to vary. Use $J$ for resultant angular momentum and impose $J=0$. Omit zero-spin faces so that adding empty faces does not generate duplicate descriptions with arbitrarily large $N$. Start with $K=2,3,4$ and every allowed $N$, then extend only as practical.

An orthonormal basis is a finite catalogue; arbitrary pure states are continuous superpositions of its vectors. Closure and $N\geq4$ do not guarantee positive volume. Record the complete closed-space dimension separately from the volume kernel and positive-volume eigenspaces.

## Implementation Details
With labelled active faces, the space of interest is

$$
\mathcal H_{\mathrm{vol}}(K)=\bigoplus_{N=4}^{2K}\;\bigoplus_{\substack{j_i>0\;\\ \sum_i j_i=K}}\operatorname{Inv}_{SU(2)}\!\left(\bigotimes_{i=1}^{N}V_{j_i}\right).
$$

Enumerate positive integer compositions $n_i=2j_i$ of $2K$ and admissible angular-momentum coupling paths. For fixed $N$, allowing zero-spin faces gives

$$
d_N(K)=\frac{1}{K+1}\binom{K+N-1}{K}\binom{K+N-2}{K}.
$$

For $K>0$, set $d_0=d_1=0$ and obtain the active-face count by

$$
d_N^+(K)=\sum_{r=0}^{N}(-1)^r\binom Nr d_{N-r}(K).
$$

| $K$ | Dimensions by active $N$ | Total for $N\geq4$ |
|---:|---|---:|
| 2 | $N=4:2$ | 2 |
| 3 | $N=4:16,\;5:15,\;6:5$ | 36 |
| 4 | $N=4:51,\;5:105,\;6:114,\;7:63,\;8:14$ | 347 |

These are closed-basis dimensions, not counts of positive-volume eigenstates. Two- and three-face closed spaces may be listed separately as degenerate controls. The existing four-face routines were reused in the variable-$N$ calculator during the October 8–9 continuation. The complete positive-face RS baseline at $K=2,3,4$ is now saved; AL remains explicitly unevaluated in this catalogue because higher-valence orientation signs are unspecified.
