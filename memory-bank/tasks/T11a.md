## Implementation Details
The baseline is the exact fixed-area SU(2)-singlet model. The four-site pilot uses connected induced triples in the positive RS volume and keeps edge–triple terms in $[H,V]$. The complete graph models equal hopping on all six tetrahedral face pairs; the ring is a sparse-hopping control. The $K=4$ candidates add positive RS volume or signed triple flux to the Bose–Hubbard baseline. Their couplings remain numerical probes until physically justified.
