## Context and Working State
The scan is for the EPJC Eq. (38) Freidel–Livine fixed-area state, with project-normalized positive RS and AL volume expectations. It covers a closed equal-face-area family only. The regular tetrahedron is the minimum in this finite grid; no all-shape minimum has been proved. The grid excludes $x=0$, $x=1$, and $\varphi=180^\circ$, so it does not settle the limits near classical zero-volume directions or their quantum volume spread.

The classical volume reconstructed from the same unit-area normals differs substantially from the quantum expectations at selected points. At the regular point, $V_{\rm cl}=0.41360216$ versus $V_{RS}=0.05077553$ and $V_{AL}=0.02538777$; at $(x,\varphi)=(0.03,15^\circ)$, $V_{\rm cl}=0.05871810$ while $V_{RS}=0.07482161$ and $V_{AL}=0.03741081$. This is an exploratory comparison, not independent classical-volume validation.

The four-ray cross ratio provides coordinates related to Thurston's four-marked-sphere moduli space, but no equivalence has been established between its cone-metric geometry and LQG flux geometry. The qhe-bhe Thurston/Minkowski material may help with classical shape constraints; it cannot by itself prove a minimum for the quantum operators.
